\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We asked whether an uploader can recover video quality beyond the resolution tier trick by
optimising a pre-compensation against a black-box platform. On simulated platforms, and judged by
metrics the optimiser never saw, the answer is: only a little, and not with a learned network. The
tier choice and a near-lossless upload carry most of the benefit, an untuned mild sharpening of natural
low-motion content adds a small, platform-dependent gain, and checking that gain on a platform costs
about 20 uploads. \pending{one sentence on the real-platform results.}
